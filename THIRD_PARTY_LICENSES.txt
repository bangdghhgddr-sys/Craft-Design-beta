THIRD-PARTY LICENSES
=====================

No third-party font files or remote font services are bundled or loaded by this build.

Craft Design uses the operating system's default font until the user imports a font file. Any imported font remains subject to its own license. The user is responsible for confirming permission to use and redistribute imported font files.

The included seven brush PNG assets are based on the developer's own hand-drawn marks supplied for this project; they are not third-party brush-pack assets.
